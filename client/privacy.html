Privacy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Gobble is a free browser game. We collect as little as possible and we never sell anything about you. There are no ads, no tracking and no advertising cookies.

What the game handles

  • Your connection (IP address). Needed to connect you to the game server. Our hosting provider (Render) may keep it in short-lived technical logs.
  • The name you type and what you do in the game (orb positions, splits, mass). This only lives in the server's memory while you play and is gone when you leave.
  • Chat messages. Shown to other players. The server keeps the last 20 public messages in memory so newcomers can follow the conversation; they disappear when the server restarts. Party messages are not kept.
  • Voice chat (only if you turn it on). Your voice goes directly between your browser and your party members' browsers, or through a free public relay server (openrelay.metered.ca) when a direct connection isn't possible. It is never recorded or stored by us. To connect, your party members' browsers can see your IP address.

Stored in your own browser

Your settings, keys, colours, battle pass progress and a copy of the last chat messages are saved in your browser's local storage so they are there next time. They never leave your device unless needed to play (for example, the colours and skin other players see). You can delete them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Services we use

  • Render (render.com) hosts the game servers in Europe and the USA.
  • Google Fonts provides the font; your browser downloads it from Google.
  • Google STUN and openrelay.metered.ca help voice chat connect.

Your rights

If you are in the EU, you have the right to ask what data we have about you and to have it deleted. Because we don't keep accounts or permanent records, there is usually nothing stored about you after you leave. To contact us, use the game's GitHub page.

Children

The game has chat and voice with other players, so it is meant for players aged 13 and over. In the EU, players under 16 need permission from a parent or guardian.
