Blobbit.

Eat. Grow. Split. Don't get eaten.

Blobbit is a free multiplayer orb game that runs in any browser, on desktop or phone. You start as a small orb, eat pellets and smaller players, split to catch prey, feed your teammates, and steer clear of the yellow needles that burst big orbs into pieces. No download, no sign-up.

Features

  • Multibox: control two orbs at once and swap between them with Tab.
  • Party codes and voice chat: share a code, join the same game and talk push-to-talk.
  • Battle pass: 30 levels of sticker skins, chat badges and screen effects, free.
  • Smart bots that hunt, split-kill and hide near needles, so the arena is never empty.
  • Arena records announced to everyone with a big banner.
  • Viewer mode to watch the whole match.
