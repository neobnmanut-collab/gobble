Who can play

You must be 13 or older. In the EU, if you are under 16 you need permission from a parent or guardian, because the game includes chat and voice with other players.

Be decent

  • No spam and no pretending to be someone else (including game staff).

Names and messages may be filtered automatically. Anyone can mute another player by clicking their name. We may remove, mute or block players who break these rules.

The game

  • Gobble is free and provided "as is". It may change, reset (for example scores, records and battle pass seasons) or be unavailable at any time.
  • Battle pass progress, skins, badges and effects are saved in your browser and have no money value.
  • There are currently no purchases in the game. If that changes, separate terms will apply.

Voice and chat

You are responsible for what you say. Voice chat only works within your party and is not recorded by us. See the privacy page for how it works.
